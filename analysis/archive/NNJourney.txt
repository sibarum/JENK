The "exactness" is just that there's no floating-point drift to hide the rationality — SGD with quadratic loss and rank-1 Hessian converges to the Gauss-Newton point in finite steps (effectively one, modulo learning rate), so you're not approximating a limit, you're computing it.

The Hessian factors as 2·(a·b)² · vvᵀ where v has integer entries. That integer-ness propagates through the closed form and pops out as rational coordinates at convergence. If you saw irrational landings, *that* would be the surprise — it would mean something non-combinatorial was leaking into the geometry.
